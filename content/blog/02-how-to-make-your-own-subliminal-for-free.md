Making your own subliminal sounds technical. It is not. You need a goal, a few sentences, a sound you find calming, and a voice. That is it.

Here is a method you can finish in one evening.

## Step 1: What is your one goal?

Start with a single theme. Not "fix my whole life," but something you can describe in a few words:

- Sleep more peacefully
- Feel more confident at work
- Speak to myself more kindly
- Stay consistent with my evening routine

One theme keeps your affirmations focused and your listening time meaningful.

## Step 2: What should the affirmations say?

Write three to eight short lines. A few quick rules:

- **Present tense.** "I am" or "I am learning to," not "I will."
- **Believable.** If you roll your eyes saying it, soften it.
- **Specific enough to picture.** "I speak calmly in meetings" beats "I am perfect."
- **Positive framing.** Say what you want, not what you want to stop.

:::example
Goal: sleep. Lines: "My body knows how to rest." "I let the day end." "I am safe and warm tonight." "I wake up gently."
:::

Stuck on wording? Our guide to writing powerful affirmations goes deeper.

## Step 3: What should it sound like underneath?

The backing layer is what makes it pleasant to return to. Options in the builder include:

- **A frequency tone.** Many people choose a Solfeggio frequency. Those associations come from cultural and spiritual traditions, not from medical research, so choose one because you like it. [[VERIFY: no controlled evidence is cited for Solfeggio frequencies; confirm wording with editorial]]
- **Ambience.** Rain, ocean or a soft hum.
- **Binaural beats or nature sounds.** Optional layers for atmosphere.

Keep it quiet enough that the voice is easy to hear. Over-layering is the most common beginner mistake.

## Step 4: Whose voice?

You have two options:

1. **Your own voice.** You record the lines. It is free, and many people find it feels more personal.
2. **A synthetic voice.** Handy if you would rather not record. In Subliminally the studio voice and voice cloning are part of the paid plan.

:::experience
A lot of first-time recorders hate the sound of their own voice. It usually fades after a few listens. Speak slowly and softly, as if reading a bedtime story to someone you love.
:::

## Step 5: How long, and how often?

Twenty minutes is a good first length and fits within the free plan's session limit. Longer sessions up to eight hours are part of the paid plan, but you do not need them to begin.

Aim for the same time each night. Consistency matters more than length.

## What do I get on the free plan?

At the time of writing, a free account lets you build a personalized subliminal with a chosen frequency and sounds, record in your own voice, and save two subliminals to your library. Details can change, so check the pricing section in the app.

## Does making my own beat downloading one?

That is a personal call. The honest advantage of making your own is **relevance**: the words are yours, so you know what you are hearing and why.
