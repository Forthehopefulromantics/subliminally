# Images

Four placeholders, generated rather than photographed — soft light in a dark
room, in the site's own palette. They're here to show where photography goes
and to keep the layouts honest until real pictures replace them.

**To swap one in: keep the filename, match the shape.** Every image sits under a
dark gradient scrim so headings stay readable whatever you put behind them, and
each is `object-fit: cover`, so it crops from the centre rather than squashing.

| File | Used on | Size | What works here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero.jpg` | Homepage, behind the orb and headline | 1600 × 1400 | Night sky, a lit window, a dark room with one warm lamp. Keep the middle uncluttered — the orb and headline sit on top of it. |
| `build.jpg` | Top of the Build page | 1600 × 900 | Headphones, a microphone, someone speaking. Detail belongs on the right; the heading sits bottom-left. |
| `ritual.jpg` | Homepage, the quote band between hero and pricing | 1600 × 640 | Wide and calm — bedding, candlelight, a horizon. A centred quote sits over it, so avoid anything busy in the middle. |
| `journal.jpg` | Top of the Rituals page | 1200 × 900 | Hands writing, an open notebook, a pen on paper. This is the one that should look like your physical journals. |

## Before you upload

- **Export at the size in the table**, or close to it. Bigger files make the
  site and the app slower to open, and the extra pixels are thrown away.
- **JPEG, quality ~75.** Aim for under 250 KB each. The placeholders are 14–23 KB.
- **Dark or mid-tone images sit best.** A bright photo will fight the scrim.
- Put them in this folder with the same names. Nothing else needs changing.

## Licensing

If you use stock rather than your own photography, check the licence allows
commercial use. Unsplash and Pexels both do. Your own photographs are better
anyway — they'll be the only ones that look like FTHR.
