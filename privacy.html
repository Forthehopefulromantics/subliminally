1. Who we are

Subliminally by FTHR ("Subliminally," "we," "us") is a wellness and personal-development app from For The Hopeful Romantics (FTHR), operated by Adaku LLC. This policy covers subliminallybyfthr.com and the Subliminally iOS and Android apps. Our Terms of Use govern everything else about the Service.

2. What we collect

Almost everything below is something you give us on purpose, by using a feature. We don't buy data about you, and we don't collect anything from you when you're not using Subliminally.

Account information

Name and email from GoogleOnly if you choose to sign in with Google instead of a password. Google tells us your email address and name; we get nothing else from your Google account, and no password.

Onboarding and Higher Self preferences

When you set up your account we ask a short series of optional questions, and store your answers on your profile so the app can speak in your language rather than someone else's:

  • The Higher Self illustration you chose and the name you gave her;
  • Up to three current desires, what you're struggling with right now, and the main outcome you want Subliminally to support — in your own words, where you typed them;
  • What role spirituality plays in your life, and what you call your faith if you chose "other." We store the name only, and nothing else about your beliefs.

These shape what things are called, which affirmations are generated, and which habits are suggested. They don't change what you can do or what anything costs, and you can skip every one of them, or change them later.

3. Your voice, your journal, and your camera

Voice recordings, spoken journal entries, photos of your journal pages, cover images and uploaded tracks are kept in private storage, in a folder belonging to your account. Access is enforced at the database level: your account can reach your folder and no one else's. Nothing is public, and there are no public links. When the app needs to play or show one of these files, it creates a temporary signed link that expires shortly afterwards.

We don't listen to your recordings, read your journal, or look at your photos. We would only ever access them if you asked us to in order to investigate a problem you reported, or if we were legally compelled to. Nothing in this category is used for anything other than giving it back to you.

The app asks for your microphone only when you choose to record — an affirmation, a spoken journal entry, or a voice sample — and for your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo of a journal page or a cover image. You can decline both and still use the rest of the app.

4. How we use your information

  • To run the Service — build and play back your sessions, keep your library, journal, habits and streaks, and remember your preferences.
  • To generate what you ask for — affirmations, EFT tapping scripts, visualization scripts and spoken audio (section 6).
  • To manage your subscription — to know which plan you're on and unlock the right features.
  • To email you about your account — confirming your address, resetting your password, and replying when you contact us.
  • To answer support requests and read the ideas you send us.
  • To keep the Service secure and working — and to comply with the law where we must.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising. We do not use your content to train AI models. We don't profile you or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

6. AI and audio processing

Two features send some of your content to a provider in order to produce what you asked for. Both are things you trigger deliberately.

Generated text

When you ask for affirmations, an EFT tapping script or a visualization script, we send Anthropic what you typed about what you want help with, along with the tone and frequency you selected. The generated lines come back to you and are stored with your session if you save it. Your name, email and account identifier are not sent, and Anthropic does not use this to train its models.

Generated speech and voice cloning

If you choose a studio voice or your own cloned voice, the line of text to be spoken is sent to ElevenLabs, which returns the audio. If you choose to clone your voice, the voice sample you record is sent to ElevenLabs, which creates a voice for your account; we store only the resulting voice identifier on your profile, not the sample. Only you can use your cloned voice. Cloning again replaces the previous voice, and the old one is deleted at the provider.

When you delete your account, or remove your cloned voice, we delete it at ElevenLabs too — a copy of your voice should not outlive the account that made it.

Affirmations recorded in your own voice, and spoken journal entries, are not sent to any AI provider. They're stored and played back as they are.

7. Analytics, cookies and local storage

Subliminally has no analytics provider. There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no third-party tracker and no session recording on the website or in the app. We don't track you across other sites or apps.

The app does note internally when you view a paywall or tap a locked feature, but those notes stay in your browser's memory for the current page only and are never transmitted anywhere.

We don't set cookies. The app uses your browser's or device's local storage for a handful of small, functional things:

  • Your signed-in session, so you're not logged out every time you come back;
  • Your plan, cached so the first screen can draw without waiting;
  • Your day/night theme preference;
  • Reflections you've already answered, and whether your device's built-in voice is unavailable, so you're not asked twice;
  • Details from a signup that hasn't finished yet, so nothing is lost between creating an account and confirming it.

Clearing your browser or app storage removes all of these, and signs you out.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep your account information and your content for as long as your account exists, because that content is the product — your library and your journal are meant to still be there next year. You can delete any individual recording, entry, photo, habit or saved subliminal from within the app at any time, and it is removed.

When you delete your account, everything described in section 9 is deleted immediately rather than kept for a retention period. Support messages and ideas you've sent us may remain in our email records. Billing and tax records held by Stripe, Apple or Google are retained by those providers under their own policies and legal obligations, and are outside our control.

9. Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from Profile → Your account → Delete my account, on the web or in the app. You don't need to email us or wait for us to act.

Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes:

  • Your voice recordings, spoken journal entries, journal photos, cover images and uploaded tracks from private storage;
  • Your habits and check-ins, journal entries, journal photos, saved subliminals and subscription record;
  • Your profile — username, Higher Self, onboarding answers and faith preference;
  • Your cloned voice, at our speech provider;
  • Your login itself, which signs you out everywhere.

This cannot be undone, and we cannot restore a deleted account. Please also note that deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it first through Stripe, the App Store or Google Play, or you may continue to be charged.

If deletion ever fails partway through, the app will tell you, and emailing hello@subliminallybyfthr.com will get it finished by hand.

10. Security

Traffic between your device and us is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and never stored in readable form. Your files sit in private storage with per-account access rules enforced by the database, and are served only through short-lived signed links. The keys that can act on any account live on our servers, never in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security — but we take reasonable and appropriate steps, and if a breach ever affected your personal information we'd notify you as the law requires.

11. Children's privacy

Subliminally is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16 — we don't do either for anyone, at any age. If you believe a child has given us information, email hello@subliminallybyfthr.com and we'll delete it promptly.

12. Your privacy rights

Wherever you live, you can exercise all of the rights below by emailing hello@subliminallybyfthr.com — we extend them to everyone rather than only where they're legally required. Most of them you can also simply do yourself in the app.

  • Know and access — what we hold about you and what we do with it. This policy says so; ask us for a copy of your data and we'll provide it.
  • Correct — fix anything inaccurate. Your profile, username, Higher Self, onboarding answers and content are all editable in the app.
  • Delete — remove your personal information. You can do this yourself (section 9).
  • Portability — receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Non-discrimination — we won't deny you service, charge you a different price, or give you a lesser experience for exercising any of these rights.

California

If you're a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA gives you the rights above, plus the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information and to opt out of sale or sharing.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising — so there is nothing to opt out of, and we have no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link because we do neither. We collect the categories described in section 2, from you, for the purposes in section 4, and disclose them to the service providers in section 5 for business purposes only. Some of what you give us — such as voice recordings and the contents of your journal — may be treated as sensitive personal information; we use it only to provide the Service back to you, and never to infer characteristics about you, which means the right to limit its use doesn't change anything about how we handle it.

You may use an authorized agent to make a request. We'll verify a request against the email on your account before acting on it, and we'll respond within the time the law allows. Other U.S. states with comprehensive privacy laws grant similar rights, and we honor them the same way.

Subliminally is operated from the United States, and your information is processed there and in other countries where our service providers operate.
